Recent dashboard updates

The eight most recent changes we have made to the information on this page, in plain English.

    Definitions
      About the data. The LAUNCH dashboard displays publicly available information only. Product-specific details (including pricing) appear solely where the manufacturer has confirmed in writing that they may be shared. Hover any stage for status detail; select a medicine to open its full profile.
      Sources. Everything on this page is traceable to one of the public sources below. Each name opens that source's own official site in a new tab, so you can check any figure yourself.
      • WHO malaria guidelines↗ Which medicines the World Health Organization recommends, and from when.
      • WHO prequalification list↗ The medicines WHO has checked for quality, safety and effectiveness. Prevention tools are on a separate list↗.
      • WHO Malaria Threat Maps↗ Where malaria is becoming harder to treat — the study results behind the resistance layers on the map above.
      • European Medicines Agency↗ European approval decisions, including its opinions on medicines meant for use outside Europe.
      • Nigeria: NAFDAC Green Book↗ The national register of every medicine approved for sale in Nigeria.
      • Tanzania: TMDA register↗ The national register of every medicine approved for sale in Tanzania.
      • Global Fund price and quality reporting↗ What was actually bought for countries — how many packs, at what price, in which year.
      • ClinicalTrials.gov↗ Trial records: how many people took part, when a trial finished, and what it found.
      • US President's Malaria Initiative Its public data portal closed in 2025, so US-funded purchases from that point on are a gap we cannot yet fill.
      • Manufacturer communications Company announcements, and figures a manufacturer has confirmed to us in writing. There is no public register for these.
      A LAUNCH initiative of Unitaid↗ partners, to be hosted by the RBM Partnership to End Malaria↗.
