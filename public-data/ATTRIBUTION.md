# Sources and attribution

The dataset is compiled from public sources. Each figure in `v1/dashboard.json`
cites its source, and `source_coverage` lists every source with its link.
The main ones and their terms:

| Source | Used for | Terms |
| --- | --- | --- |
| World Health Organization: Guidelines for malaria; Prequalification lists; World Malaria Report 2025, Annex 4B (antimalarial drug policy) | Recommendations, prequalification, national treatment policy (`treatmentPolicy`) | CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 IGO. WHO does not endorse this dataset. |
| European Medicines Agency: EU-M4all / Article 58 opinions | Regulatory approval | Reuse with attribution to EMA |
| The Global Fund: Price & Quality Reporting; Data Service | Procurement volumes and spend | Global Fund Terms of Use; attribute the Global Fund |
| ClinicalTrials.gov (US National Library of Medicine) | Trial records | US public domain |
| National medicine registers: NAFDAC Greenbook (Nigeria), TMDA (Tanzania) | Country registrations | Public government registers; attributed |
| MMV, manufacturers' announcements, peer-reviewed studies | Pipeline status, study results | As cited per figure |

**Licence of this dataset: to be decided before handover.** Parts of it are
adapted from WHO material licensed CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 IGO, whose ShareAlike term
applies to adaptations, and whose NonCommercial term limits reuse. The licence
chosen for this repository has to be compatible with that.

French and Portuguese text is machine-translated (Google Cloud Translation)
from the English unless noted otherwise; the English is the reference.
